ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Practical learning for work and enterprise.

VOCATIONAL TRAINING CENTER PHÓTIZÓ SOLAR_QHSE INSTITUTE (VTC-PSQHSE) is a private vocational training institute registered under the Ministry of Employment and Vocational Training (MINEFOP).

Based in Bamenda, the institute provides professional, practical and market-oriented training in renewable energy, solar power system installation, quality management, occupational health and safety, and environmental management.

Training combines instructor guidance, classroom learning and practical exposure. The aim is to help learners develop skills they can apply in employment and self-employment.

The institute issues a Certificate of Completion at the conclusion of each training session. This is not a claim of an external professional certification or ISO certification.

ACADEMIC & PROFESSIONAL PROGRAMMES

Explore our training programmes.

Programmes cover practical renewable energy skills and key QHSE management subjects. Contact the Academy to confirm the next intake, course schedule and delivery format.

PROGRAMME 01

Renewable Energy – Solar Power System Installation

Practical training in solar system design and sizing, panels, inverters, battery storage, hybrid and off-grid systems, solar water pumping, maintenance and safe electrical installation.

PROGRAMME 02

Occupational Health & Safety Management Systems (ISO 45001)

Learn hazard identification, risk assessment, control measures, safety procedures, incident investigation and workplace safety management.

PROGRAMME 03

Quality Management Systems (ISO 9001)

Understand quality principles, process-based management, documentation, performance indicators, internal audits and corrective actions.

PROGRAMME 04

Environmental Management Systems (ISO 14001)

Study environmental aspects and impacts, waste management, pollution prevention, environmental protection and sustainability practices.

PROGRAMME 05

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Fundamentals

An introduction to AI concepts and practical applications, including how data, algorithms and automation support tasks in modern industries.

LEARNING APPROACH

Learn the concepts.
Practise the skills.

Solar training includes exposure to actual solar equipment such as panels, batteries, inverters and installation tools. Depending on the programme, learning may include demonstrations, supervised practical sessions, safety exercises and workplace documentation.

  • Solar system design and sizing
  • Panel, inverter and battery installation concepts
  • Hybrid and off-grid system configuration
  • Solar water pumping principles
  • Maintenance and troubleshooting
  • Workplace safety and risk assessment

COMMON QUESTIONS

Academy FAQs.

Where is PHÓTIZÓ Solar Academy located?

The training centre is in Ntsuabub, directly opposite the entrance to the Presbyterian Church, Bamenda, Cameroon.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply for Admission button to open the existing Google Form. You can also read the admission instructions page for guidance on opening the form.

What is the published fee?

The current schedule lists 15,000 FCFA registration, 25,000 FCFA first instalment and 15,000 FCFA final instalment, totalling 55,000 FCFA.

What certificate is issued?

The Academy issues its own Certificate of Completion at the conclusion of each session. Do not interpret this as an external ISO certification or government-issued qualification.
