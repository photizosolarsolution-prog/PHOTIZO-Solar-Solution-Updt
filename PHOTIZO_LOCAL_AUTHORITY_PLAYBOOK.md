# PHOTIZO Local Authority Playbook

## Purpose
Strengthen PHOTIZO Solar Solutions and PHOTIZO Solar Academy as discoverable, trustworthy local organisations. This plan is designed around Google's published local ranking factors: relevance, distance and prominence. No agency or tool can guarantee a particular ranking.

Check and correct:
- Exact public business name: PHÓTIZÓ Solar Solutions (use the real-world name consistently; do not add keyword stuffing).
- Primary and additional categories: select only categories that accurately describe the actual business and are available in Google's category picker.
- Address: Ntsuabub, directly opposite the entrance to the Presbyterian Church, Bamenda, Cameroon.
- Main phone: +237 679 59 08 76. Secondary phone: +237 698 33 65 48.
- Website: https://photizosolar.com/
- Business hours: enter only the actual hours customers can visit or contact the business.
- Services: solar system design, solar installation, solar equipment sales, solar audits/consultations, solar generators, maintenance/after-sales support and solar water pumping where offered.
- Business description: describe the solar division and the vocational training division accurately. Keep the description natural and factual.
- Photos/videos: upload genuine PHOTIZO premises, team, installations, equipment, training sessions and project photos. Add captions where appropriate; do not use stock photos as evidence of completed work.
- Website and contact links: use the matching official domain and active contact details.

Service area: Bamenda is the physical base. Add other towns only where PHOTIZO genuinely serves customers. Douala may be described as an area PHOTIZO accepts project enquiries from; do not imply a staffed branch or office there unless one exists.

## 2. Name, address and phone consistency
Use the same public-facing business name, Bamenda address, phone numbers and website on the website, Google Business Profile, Facebook, LinkedIn and any legitimate local directories. Correct outdated Gmail addresses on owned pages where practical; keep professional email addresses on the main domain.

## 3. Genuine customer and trainee reviews
- Ask every customer after a completed installation, repair or consultation for an honest review.
- Ask trainees after a course or training session for honest feedback.
- Use Google's review link or QR code from the verified profile.
- Never offer discounts, gifts or payments for reviews; never ask only satisfied customers; never write reviews on customers' behalf.
- Reply politely to reviews, including critical feedback, without exposing private customer details.

Suggested neutral request:
"Thank you for choosing PHÓTIZÓ. If you have a moment, please share an honest review of your experience. Your feedback helps other customers understand our services and helps us improve. [Google review link]"

## 4. Regular evidence of real work
Publish a steady stream of authentic updates on the profile and owned social channels:
- Completed solar installations (with customer permission).
- Before-and-after maintenance or upgrade work.
- The Ntamuche community solar water-pump rehabilitation and upgrade.
- Product demonstrations with verified model names and specifications.
- Practical Academy sessions, facilities and trainee work (with consent).
- Admission dates and course information only when confirmed current.

Do not upload images of customers, trainees or identifiable premises without appropriate permission.

## 5. Local links and mentions
Seek genuine mentions and links from organisations that have actually worked with PHOTIZÓ:
- Suppliers and manufacturers, where PHOTIZÓ is an authorised or genuine reseller/installer and the supplier is willing to list it.
- Community organisations, schools, farms, NGOs or institutions involved in real projects.
- Training partners, employers, professional associations and event organisers.
- Local business associations and credible Cameroon business directories.
- Local news outlets covering real community energy or training projects.

Prioritise useful case studies and partner pages over mass directory submissions or paid link schemes. Never claim a partnership, certification or award that cannot be substantiated.

## 6. Content programme
Publish genuinely useful articles that answer questions PHOTIZÓ hears from customers and students. Initial topics:
1. How to estimate the right solar system size for a home in Cameroon.
2. Battery capacity versus inverter power: what buyers should understand.
3. Solar water pumping: the information needed before choosing a pump.
4. How to maintain a solar installation during the rainy season.
5. What students learn in practical solar installation training.
6. Basic workplace electrical safety for solar installations.

Each article should include original PHOTIZÓ experience, real photographs where available, an author or reviewer, useful internal links and a clear contact action. Do not publish thin, repetitive pages made only to target town names.

## 7. Review and measurement cadence
Monthly, review Google Business Profile performance and Search Console data. Track calls, website clicks, direction requests, search queries, impressions, clicks and the pages generating enquiries. Compare trends over several months rather than judging a new page after a few days.
