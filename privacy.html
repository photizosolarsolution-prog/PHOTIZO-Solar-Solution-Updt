YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

This notice explains how PHÓTIZÓ Solar Solutions and PHÓTIZÓ Vocational Training Centre handle information when you visit this website or contact us through the options provided here.

Information submitted through this website

The website enquiry form does not send information to a PHÓTIZÓ website database. When you submit it, your device opens WhatsApp with a message you can review and send. Information you send through WhatsApp is handled by WhatsApp and is also received by the PHÓTIZÓ contact account.

Admissions and external services

The admissions link currently opens an external Google Form. Information submitted there is handled through Google Forms and the associated PHÓTIZÓ admissions workflow. Google and WhatsApp have their own privacy terms and practices. Please review those services' notices before submitting personal information.

How information may be used

Information voluntarily sent to PHÓTIZÓ may be used to respond to enquiries, assess admissions applications, communicate about training or solar services, administer services requested, and maintain relevant business or training records.

Access and protection

PHÓTIZÓ should limit access to applicant, trainee and customer information to personnel who need it for their assigned responsibilities, and use appropriate safeguards when storing, sharing or retaining those records.

Your questions

For questions about information you have submitted directly to PHÓTIZÓ, contact photizosolarsolution@gmail.com or call +237 679 59 08 76.

Important: This is a basic website privacy notice, not a complete legal compliance assessment. Before launch, PHÓTIZÓ should review it against its actual data-handling practices and applicable Cameroon requirements.
