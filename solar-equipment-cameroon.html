BUILD A COMPLETE SYSTEM

Components work best when designed together.

01 · GENERATION

Solar panels

Panel quantity and capacity depend on available sunlight, roof or ground space, system voltage and daily energy demand.

02 · STORAGE

Batteries

Battery chemistry, usable capacity and autonomy should match the loads and the required backup duration.

03 · CONVERSION

Inverters

Inverter selection considers continuous output, surge requirements, battery voltage and the type of loads.

04 · CONTROL

Charge controllers

Controller type and rating must be compatible with the solar array and battery configuration.

05 · PROTECTION

Electrical protection

Correctly selected breakers, isolators, surge protection, cables and earthing are important parts of a safe installation.

06 · SUPPORT

After-sales care

System checks, maintenance and technical support help owners understand and care for their equipment.

Buy for your actual energy needs.

A portable power station, backup system and full off-grid installation serve different needs. Before selecting a product, consider what you will connect, the total running wattage, starting surge, expected runtime, charging options and whether the product is intended for indoor or outdoor use.
