ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Professional skills.
Practical impact.

VOCATIONAL TRAINING CENTER PHÓTIZÓ SOLAR_QHSE INSTITUTE (VTC-PSQHSE) is an authorized private vocational training institute duly registered under the Ministry of Employment and Vocational Training (MINEFOP).

Established to provide professional, practical and market-oriented training, the institute offers solar installation training, renewable energy education and occupational health and safety training in Cameroon. Our programmes equip learners with practical skills for careers in solar power system installation, quality management systems, environmental management systems and workplace safety.

OUR VISION

To become a reference vocational training center in Quality, Occupational Health & Safety, Environmental Management, and Renewable Energy in Cameroon; a center of excellence empowering professionals to promote sustainable energy solutions while ensuring safe and healthy work environments.

OUR MISSION

To provide high-quality, practical and industry-relevant market-oriented vocational training that promotes quality assurance, workplace safety, environmental protection, and the development of renewable energy skills for employment and self-employment.

FEATURED PROJECT · NTAMUCHE COMMUNITY

Solar water-pump rehabilitation and upgrade

The original solar water system operated only during daylight hours. Its limited solar-panel capacity could not adequately meet the community's water demand. PHÓTIZÓ upgraded the system with a 3kVA hybrid solar inverter, a 2.4kWh lithium battery, additional solar panels and an automatic float switch. The upgrade was designed to support more dependable water availability beyond daylight hours.
