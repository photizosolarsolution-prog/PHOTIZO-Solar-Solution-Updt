SYSTEM DESIGN

Why proper sizing matters.

A solar system should be designed around the loads it is expected to serve. An undersized system may not meet demand; an unnecessarily oversized system can increase project cost.

Our design discussions consider daytime and night-time use, essential and non-essential loads, battery autonomy, available roof or ground space and future expansion plans.

Prepare for a useful consultation

Before contacting us, make a list of the appliances you want to power, their wattage if known, how many hours they run each day, your town or neighbourhood and whether you need backup during grid outages.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Solar installation FAQs.

Can PHOTIZÓ install solar systems for homes and businesses?

Yes. PHOTIZÓ provides solar system design and installation services for residential, commercial and institutional applications. The system design depends on the site's energy requirements.

How much does a solar installation cost in Cameroon?
